# Generative AI Usage Policy in Updainium

The use of generative AI tools for any work on or communication about Updainium is prohibited.

Generative AI tools must not be used to create, modify, translate, summarize, or review source code, documentation, issues, comments, pull requests, or any other project-related content.

All contributions and project-related communications must be created and reviewed by people without generative AI assistance. Automated accounts or agents must not publish or submit content to the project.

Contributions that violate this policy may be closed or rejected, and participating accounts may be blocked.
